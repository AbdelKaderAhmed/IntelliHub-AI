IntelliHub AI

Vision

IntelliHub AI is an enterprise-grade AI Knowledge Platform designed for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs).
The platform transforms company documents into a secure, searchable, AI-powered knowledge system that enables employees to interact with organizational knowledge using natural language.

Problem Statement

SMEs store critical knowledge across PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, contracts, policies, reports, and manuals.
Finding information is time-consuming and knowledge is often siloed.

Solution

IntelliHub AI provides:

Document ingestion

AI-powered search

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Source citations

Knowledge analytics

Secure multi-tenant architecture
